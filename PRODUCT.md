# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Retinova serves screening staff, teachers and competition judges, and people receiving preliminary retinal screening. The primary operator uploads a retinal fundus photograph, checks whether the image is usable, reviews an AI screening result, and decides whether the person should seek an ophthalmologist. It is not an iris-identification product and is not a replacement for clinical diagnosis.

## Product Purpose

Retinova is an explainable retinal fundus screening prototype. It should make the distinction between measured model output, explanatory visualization, and clinical follow-up unmistakable. Success means a reviewer can understand the result in seconds, inspect the evidence and limitations, and trace every public metric to a reproducible evaluation artifact.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, accountable. The voice is confident without hype, medically literate without intimidating non-specialists, and transparent whenever a capability is experimental or unavailable.

## Anti-references

- Generic glassmorphism medical dashboards that use decoration to imply scientific credibility.
- Autonomous-diagnosis claims, invented percentages, or heatmaps that are not generated by the model.
- Dense hospital software that hides the primary screening decision behind navigation and widgets.
- Consumer wellness language that treats a screening result as a diagnosis.

## Design Principles

1. Show the screening decision first, then the evidence and limitation.
2. Separate measured outputs from rules, templates, and illustrative content.
3. Make uncertainty actionable: unreadable image, unavailable model, and low confidence are explicit outcomes.
4. Keep the clinician in control; referral and confirmation language appears with every result.
5. Every metric, model name, dataset claim, and visualization must be traceable to an artifact or source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. All controls must work by keyboard, focus must remain visible, text contrast must meet 4.5:1, status must never depend on color alone, animations must respect reduced-motion preferences, and Thai and English content must remain understandable at large text sizes.
